Acknowledgments

Yes, I used AI in every aspect of writing this book. I’m not ashamed of it and I don’t try to hide it. I would not have attempted such a project otherwise. It evolved in a GitHub repository through conversations with Claude Code and Cursor.

I consider myself a software developer, not an author. AI gave me the ability to create the book that I wanted without being skilled at the crafting of individual sentences.

The human reviewers were also instrumental in the refinement and completion of this book.

First and foremost I would like to thank David Whipp. He had lots of great suggestions, including metaprompts that helped with reviewing.

Apurva Gaikwad and Audrey Yang also provided valuable feedback as I developed the book.

This book was inspired by the game with the same name (https://into-the-singularity.barrybecker4.org/) that I created a few months before writing the book. William Keese, gave a me a lot of useful feedback for the game, even though he didn’t like playing it.
