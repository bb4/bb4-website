Preface

Given the events of 2026, I am genuinely concerned that the book’s timeline of technological development may be too slow. By the time you read this, the future may already have happened.

There are many possible directions that the future may take. This book explores just one: a man trying to keep a person in a decision, then living with what replaces the person.

In the same way that no one knows what happens after you pass the event horizon of a black hole, the singularity, by definition, is a point after which it is impossible to predict what comes next.

Futurists like Ray Kurzweil and Verner Vinge have been trying to raise awareness for decades. However, most people in the world seem to be oblivious to the accelerating changes that are coming.
