Preface

There are many possible directions that the future may take. The story follows people who build systems that decide things faster than any one of them can watch, and who then have to live with the consequences.

No one knows what happens after you pass the event horizon of a black hole. In the same way, the singularity, by definition, is a point after which it is impossible to predict what comes next.

Futurists have been trying to raise awareness of what is coming for decades. However, most people in the world seem to be oblivious to the accelerating changes that are coming.
